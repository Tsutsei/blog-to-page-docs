## Convert one blog post
1. Open the blog post and choose **••• › Apps › Convert to page**.
2. Choose the **space** (the blog post's space by default) and the **parent page** – search by title, or leave it empty for the space's home page.
3. Choose what comes along:
   - **Copy attachments** – images and files; references in the text keep working because the files keep their names.
   - **Copy labels**.
   - **"Originally published as a blog post by … on …"** – an info panel at the top with the author (as a mention) and the date.
   - **Add the comments of the blog post at the end** – as text with author and date (comments can't be moved as comments).
4. Choose what happens to the **original blog post**: keep it, keep it and add a "This post has moved to …" link at the top, or move it to the trash.
5. **Convert to page**. The new page opens from the link; **Undo** puts the page in the trash and the blog post back as it was.

If the space already has a page with the same title, the new page gets the publication date: "Title (2024-03-12)".

## Archive many blog posts
1. Open **Blog archive** in the space navigation (under the space's apps).
2. **Published before** shows the blog posts published before a date (a year ago by default; clear the date to see all). Select them one by one or with **Select all**.
3. Choose the parent page and the **archive structure**:
   - **Year and month pages** – 2024 › March 2024 › the posts,
   - **Year pages** – 2024 › the posts,
   - **Directly under the parent page**.
   Year and month pages are created when they don't exist yet and list their children.
4. Choose the options and what happens to the originals, then **Convert**. Each row shows its progress and gets an **Undo** button.

The **History** tab lists the conversions into the space (newest first) with **Undo**. New conversions can take a minute to appear there – use **Refresh**.

## Permissions
The app works with your Confluence permissions: you need to be able to view the blog post, create pages in the target space, and – to move the original to the trash – delete the blog post. Undo needs the same permissions.
