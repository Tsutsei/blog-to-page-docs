**Blog to Page** turns Confluence blog posts into pages – one at a time, or hundreds at once into a tidy archive.

Confluence can't convert a blog post into a page, and old blog posts pile up in the blog list forever. Teams publish news, release notes or meeting summaries as blog posts and later want them in the page tree, next to the documentation they belong to. Blog to Page:

- **Converts a blog post to a page** – from the blog post's ••• › Apps menu: choose the space and the parent page. Attachments and labels come along, images and file links keep working, and an "Originally published as a blog post by … on …" note keeps the history. Optionally the comments are added at the end.
- **Archives blog posts in bulk** – on the space's **Blog archive** page: pick the blog posts published before a date and move them into **year and month pages** (2024 › March 2024) under a parent of your choice.
- **Takes care of the original** – keep it, keep it with a "this post has moved" link at the top, or move it to the trash.
- **Undoes every conversion** – the page goes to the trash and the blog post comes back as it was.

## Safe by design
- Everything is done **with the user's own permissions** – people can only convert what they could copy and delete by hand.
- The app stores only a short history of conversions (ids, titles, date) for undo – no page content and no personal data. **Runs on Atlassian.**
