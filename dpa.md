_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the [Terms of use]({{ '/terms/' | relative_url }}) between the customer installing **Blog to Page – Convert & Archive Blog Posts for Confluence** ("Customer", controller) and **Bc. Tomáš Buchta, sole trader, IČO 21540951, 30. dubna 2307/12, 702 00 Ostrava, Czech Republic** ("Vendor", processor), and applies to personal data processed by the App under Article 28 of the GDPR.

1. **Subject matter and duration.** Copying the Customer's Confluence blog posts into pages within the Customer's site and keeping a short conversion history, for as long as the App is installed.
2. **Nature and purpose.** Reading a blog post with the user's permissions, creating a page with its content, attachments, labels and optionally comments, changing or trashing the original when chosen, and storing ids and titles for undo.
3. **Types of personal data.** Personal data contained in the copied content (e.g. names, mentions, comment authors), processed inside Confluence and not stored by the App. The stored history contains ids, titles and dates only.
4. **Data subjects.** People mentioned in or authoring the Customer's blog posts and comments.
5. **Instructions.** The Vendor processes personal data only to operate the App as used by the Customer and on documented instructions sent to [support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net](mailto:support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net).
6. **Where data is processed.** Exclusively within Atlassian's Forge platform (Key-Value Store) for the Customer's site, inheriting Atlassian's data residency. The App sends no data outside Atlassian. The Vendor has no access to the stored data.
7. **Sub-processor.** Atlassian (provider of the Forge platform), under Atlassian's own terms and data processing addendum. The Vendor will announce any new sub-processor on this page in advance.
8. **Security.** Data is protected by the security measures of the Atlassian Forge platform (encryption at rest and in transit, tenant isolation). Content is copied with the user's own permissions and never stored by the App.
9. **Confidentiality.** Only the Vendor works on the App and is bound by confidentiality.
10. **Assistance.** The Vendor assists the Customer with data subject requests and data protection obligations as far as the App's design allows (content is managed in Confluence; the history can be removed by uninstalling the App).
11. **Personal data breaches.** The Vendor notifies the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a breach affecting the App.
12. **Deletion.** The history keeps the latest 200 conversions per space. On uninstallation, Atlassian deletes the App's data according to the Forge data retention policy.
13. **Audits.** The Vendor makes available the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA on request.
14. **Governing law.** As in the Terms of use.
