This policy describes how the Atlassian Marketplace app "Blog to Page – Convert & Archive Blog Posts for Confluence" (the "App") processes data. The App is provided by **Bc. Tomáš Buchta, sole trader, Company ID (IČO) 21540951, registered office 30. dubna 2307/12, 702 00 Ostrava, Czech Republic** ("we"). Contact: **[support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net](mailto:support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net)**.

## 1. What the App stores
A short **history of conversions** for undo: the ids and titles of the blog post and the new page, the space id, what happened to the original blog post and the date (at most 200 entries per space). The App stores **no page or blog content** and **no account ids, names or e-mail addresses**.

## 2. What the App reads and changes
When a user converts a blog post, the App reads the blog post (content, attachments, labels and – if chosen – comments) and creates a page with this content **in the user's Confluence site, with that user's permissions**. If chosen, it adds a note to the original blog post or moves it to the trash. Content is copied inside Confluence; it is never sent to or stored by the App's backend.

## 3. Where the data is stored
The conversion history is stored in Atlassian's infrastructure (Forge Key-Value Store) and inherit Atlassian's data residency. The App does not send data to any server outside Atlassian, and we (the vendor) have no access to it.

## 4. Purpose and legal basis
Data is processed solely to provide the App's functions on behalf of the Confluence site, which acts as the controller; we act as processor (Art. 28 GDPR) where personal data is involved (for example names or mentions in the content that is copied).

## 5. Retention
The history keeps the latest 200 conversions per space; older entries are deleted. When the App is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes the App's data according to its Forge data retention policy.

## 6. Data Processing Addendum
The [Data Processing Addendum]({{ '/dpa/' | relative_url }}) (Art. 28 GDPR) applies to all customers.

## 7. Sharing
We do not sell or share data. Sub-processor: Atlassian (hosting of the Forge platform).

## 8. Your rights
Content stays in Confluence and is managed there. For requests concerning personal data, contact your Confluence site admin or us at [support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net](mailto:support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net).
